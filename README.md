Stryvur – AI-Powered Adaptive Fitness Coaching

The Problem
Professional fitness coaching costs $300–600/month and remains out of reach for most Americans. Meanwhile, only 33% of U.S. adults track deeper health indicators like sleep patterns or blood pressure. No single platform today unifies wearable health data, biomarker testing, personalized training, and nutrition coaching into one intelligent experience.

The Solution
Stryvur delivers AI-powered adaptive workout plans that learn and adjust based on your body's actual state—not just your calendar. Your fitness plan evolves with you.

Key Features
🏃 Adaptive Workouts – AI adjusts intensity, volume, and recovery based on sleep quality, soreness, and real-time heart rate
📱 Apple Health Integration – Seamless data sync from wearables (Apple Watch, etc.) with zero extra work
🥗 Personalized Nutrition – Daily guidance tailored to your goals, metabolism, and preferences
✅ Daily Check-ins – Smart health tracking that learns your patterns over time
🎮 Gamification – Streaks, milestones, and rewards that drive consistency
💪 Biomarker Insights – Understand your sleep, recovery, and performance metrics in one place

Getting Started
Prerequisites
iOS 16+ or Android 12+
Apple Health app (for wearable sync)
Basic health profile setup
Installation

Tech Stack
Frontend: React Native / Flutter
Backend: Node.js / Python FastAPI
AI/ML: TensorFlow, scikit-learn (workout adaptation)
Health Data: Apple HealthKit API, FHIR standards
Database: PostgreSQL, Redis (caching)
Infrastructure: AWS / GCP
